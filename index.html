B2B BUYING & SHIPPING ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions & Wholesale Terms

Everything you need to know about ordering directly from Paradise Wholesale Clothing in Istanbul, Turkey.

1. What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and how does the 25+ pcs wholesale rate work? ▼
We support both new boutiques and large retail chains. You can order starting from just 1 Serie Pack (1 full size run of a single model, e.g., S-M-L-XL) to inspect our fabric weight, stitching, and fit in person. When your cart reaches 25 total pieces or more (roughly 4 to 5 packs across any categories), our Best Wholesale Bulk Tier Rate is unlocked automatically for maximum retail profit margin.
2. How fast is shipping from Istanbul to Italy, Spain, the UK, USA, and Europe? ▼
All in-stock serie packs ship directly from our Istanbul hub within 24 hours. Express air couriers (DHL Express, UPS, FedEx) and dedicated customs-cleared B2B cargo lines deliver to Italy (Milan, Rome, Naples), Spain (Madrid, Barcelona), the UK, Germany, and France in 3 to 5 business days, and to the United States & Canada in 4 to 6 business days with live tracking numbers.
3. How can I search for specific luxury styles or brand-inspired collections? ▼
Simply use the Live Search Bar at the top of the page! Type any style keyword, SKU code, or specific brand tag to instantly reveal matching items with their full model titles and pack prices. You can also click the WhatsApp button to request our private VIP Telegram & WhatsApp catalog directly from our multilingual sales specialists.
4. How does the "Send Order on WhatsApp" digital invoice process work? ▼
Add your desired serie packs to the Wholesale Order Bag. Our calculator automatically multiplies the unit price by the number of pieces per pack. When you click "Send Order on WhatsApp", our system generates a permanent digital order link (e.g., paradisewholesaler.com/order.html?id=ORD-8492) containing HD photos and quantities, and opens a direct chat with our Istanbul sales desk to confirm shipping costs and payment.
5. Why source from Paradise Turkey Wholesale (Istanbul Merter & Güngören Manufacturer Hub)? ▼
Paradise Wholesale Clothing (Paradise Turkey Wholesale) bridges Istanbul's world-renowned textile manufacturing districts directly with international fashion retailers. Unlike middlemen, we provide factory-direct pricing on heavyweight combed cotton t-shirts, luxury fleece tracksuits, tailored European women's wear, handcrafted leather shoes, and designer-inspired bags—backed by strict piece-by-piece quality control before dispatch.
